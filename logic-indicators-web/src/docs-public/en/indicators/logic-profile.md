# Logic Profile

> **Public overview** — for the complete technical manual, please [sign in to your account](/login?next=/dashboard/docs/indicators/logic-profile).

## What is it?

The **Logic Profile** indicator is an institutional-grade charting tool that maps traded volume, delta, and time (TPO) across specific price levels over a given period. Instead of seeing what happened inside a single candle, you see the macroscopic auction process over an entire session, week, or custom-drawn area.

By identifying the Point of Control (POC), Value Area (VA), and Initial Balance (IB), you can pinpoint exactly where the market found fair value and where institutional participants are likely to defend their positions in the future.

## Key features

- **Volume & Delta Profiles** as horizontal Bars or smooth Geometry
- **TPO (Market Profile)** with Letters, Blocks, or both
- **Value Area (VAH / VAL)** highlighting the 70% concentration zone
- **Point of Control (POC)** marking the highest-volume price level
- **Initial Balance (IB)** vertical line for the first X minutes of the session
- **Naked / Infinity line extensions** that auto-cut the moment price touches them

## Who is it for?

Intraday and swing traders who want to trade around the market's auction levels instead of guessing where support and resistance sit.
