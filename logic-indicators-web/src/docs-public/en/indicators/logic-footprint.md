# Logic Footprint

> **Public overview** — for the complete technical manual (every parameter, every configuration option, best practices), please [sign in to your account](/login?next=/dashboard/docs/indicators/logic-footprint).

## What is it?

The **Logic Footprint** indicator transforms every candle on your chart into a detailed map of executed volume. Instead of just seeing Open, High, Low, Close, you see how many contracts traded at each price level inside that bar — and whether the buyers or sellers were aggressive at each tick.

This is the foundation of Order Flow analysis: it lets you spot absorption, exhaustion, and aggressive initiative that are completely invisible in a standard candlestick chart.

## Key features

- **Volume + Delta per price level** inside every candle
- **Bid/Ask imbalance visualization** with heatmap colors
- **Stacked imbalances** to detect institutional absorption zones
- **Unfinished Business (UBA / DNBA)** markers for continuation setups
- **Volume filters** to hide low-activity noise

## Who is it for?

Day traders, scalpers, and order flow enthusiasts who need to read the auction process at the tick level instead of guessing from candle shapes alone.
