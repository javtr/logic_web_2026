# Logic Composite

> **Public overview** — for the complete technical manual, please [sign in to your account](/login?next=/dashboard/docs/indicators/logic-composite).

## What is it?

The **Logic Composite** indicator is built for macro-structural analysis. While the standard Logic Profile is perfect for session-by-session breakdowns, the Composite is designed to analyze massive chunks of historical data or specific, manually drawn areas of consolidation.

Build "Composite Profiles" that span multiple days, weeks, or months and pin them to the side of the screen to identify major historical support and resistance zones that standard intraday profiles simply cannot see.

## Key features

- **Macro Data Columns** spanning weeks or months of history
- **Fixed Screen Anchors** to the Left or Right edge that stay visible while you scroll
- **Manual Drawing Mode** (similar to a Fibonacci retracement) for custom swing analysis
- **Value Area & POC** calculated on macro datasets as major historical boundaries
- **Metrics Panel** summarizing total Volume, Delta, and Range of the entire composite structure

## Who is it for?

Position traders and macro analysts who need to identify the dominant historical levels where the market has defended its positions over weeks or months.
