# Logic Composite

> **The macro Volume, Delta, and TPO Profile architecture docked directly onto your chart.**  
> *Uncover true market structure across the long term: consolidate weeks, months, or custom swings into synchronized dual-column profiles without losing sight of intraday price action.*

---

## What Problem Does It Solve?

The vast majority of retail traders struggle because they trade blind to the broader market context:

1. **The Short-Term Trap:** Trading exclusively on 1-minute or 5-minute charts makes local swing levels seem massive, when in reality price is running straight into an institutional volume wall established 3 weeks prior that the trader cannot see.
2. **Platform Freezes on Heavy Historical Data:** Attempting to render a 60- or 90-day Volume Profile in NinjaTrader typically causes platform stutter, frozen charts, or candlestick misalignment.
3. **Lost Visual Reference When Scrolling:** Conventional profiles shift, stretch, or disappear when you scroll back in time, forcing continuous manual zooming and losing sight of current price relative to macro value.

### The Logic Composite Solution

Docks a permanent institutional profile onto the right or left margin of your screen with a fixed pixel width, regardless of how you pan through historical candles. It condenses months of trading into **two customizable synchronized columns** (e.g., Total Volume + Delta, or Volume + TPO Market Profile), projecting authentic institutional Points of Control (**POC**) and Value Area extremes (**VAH / VAL**) across your entire chart into the past and future.

---

## What Exactly Is the Indicator?

**Logic Composite** is a multi-session structural profiling station for NinjaTrader 8. Engineered for medium- and long-term auction analysis, it computes and consolidates volume distribution, net delta bias, and Time-Price-Opportunity (TPO / Market Profile) across any chosen timeframe: from visible screen candles to days, weeks, months back, or interactively drawn swings. Its exclusive **synchronized dual-column architecture** and **full-screen extension lines (*ScreenLeft*)** turn your intraday chart into a macro strategic roadmap of institutional supply and demand.

---

## Key Features

* **Fixed Screen Margin Docking:** Stays cleanly anchored to the right or left margin of your workspace with a fixed pixel width, preserving your macro reference levels as you freely navigate through intraday candles.
* **Synchronized Dual-Column Architecture:** Displays two complementary analytical lenses side by side within the same profile (e.g., Column 1 with structural *Volume Profile* and Column 2 with *Delta Profile* or *TPO Market Profile* with letters and blocks).
* **7 Flexible Range Modes:** Choose between visible candles (`VisibleBars`), all loaded history (`AllLoadedBars`), days back (`DaysBack`), weeks back (`WeeksBack`), months back (`MonthsBack`), a fixed date (`CustomDate`), or mouse-drawn swings (`ManualDraw`).
* **Full-Screen Extension Lines (*ScreenLeft*):** Projects macro POC, VAH, and VAL levels horizontally across the entire chart window to the left border, allowing surgical visibility of how current candles react to levels formed weeks ago.
* **Dual Visualization: Classic Bars or Smooth Geometry:** Switch between traditional horizontal bar histograms or high-definition smoothed polygon contours highlighting High Volume Nodes (HVN) and Low Volume Nodes (LVN) instantly.
* **Macro TPO Market Profile with Heatmaps:** Groups customizable time brackets (e.g., 30, 60, or 240 minutes) with letter, block, or chronological thermal color gradients to audit the temporal progression of the macro auction.
* **Integrated Quantitative Metrics Panel:** Collapsible statistical summary docked at the top or bottom displaying Total Volume, Accumulated Delta, Tick Range, and key structural price levels.
* **Ultra-Fast Historical Engine:** Aggregated data loading modes built to crunch weeks or months of tick history in seconds without platform lag or frame drops.

## Who Is It For?

* **Swing Traders & Position Traders:** Who base decisions on multi-week or multi-month balances and need exact institutional inflection zones.
* **Day Traders & Scalpers:** Who need a macro compass on their execution chart to avoid buying at the high of prior weeks' profiles or selling directly above an institutional POC.
* **Auction Market Theory (AMT) Specialists:** Who combine volume acceptance with time distribution (TPO) to identify transitions between macro balance and imbalance.
