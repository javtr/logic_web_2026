# Logic Footer

> **Public overview** — for the complete technical manual, please [sign in to your account](/login?next=/dashboard/docs/indicators/logic-footer).

## What is it?

The **Logic Footer** indicator is a powerful statistical engine that sits at the bottom of your chart (or floats above/below your candles) and extracts the raw Order Flow data of every single bar: Cumulative Delta, Commitment of Traders (COT), Max/Min Delta, and Trade Counts.

By translating complex tick data into an easy-to-read, color-coded grid, you can instantly spot divergences, evaluate the true effort behind a breakout, and monitor the shifting momentum of the session.

## Key features

- **Fixed Footer:** traditional statistical grid locked at the bottom of the chart, perfect for tracking Cumulative Delta and Session Volume.
- **Floating DataBox:** compact box that hovers above or below the candles, ideal for monitoring immediate bar metrics while keeping your eyes on price.
- **Dynamic Heatmaps:** cell backgrounds change color or opacity based on how strong the value is compared to the rest of the market.
- **Auto-Fit Engine:** automatically compresses the chart's price scale so the candles never hide behind the panel.

## Who is it for?

Traders who want continuous, at-a-glance statistics on every bar without needing to expand tooltips or open secondary windows.
