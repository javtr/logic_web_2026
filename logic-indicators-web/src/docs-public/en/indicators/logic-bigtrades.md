# Logic BigTrades

> **Public overview** — for the complete technical manual, please [sign in to your account](/login?next=/dashboard/docs/indicators/logic-bigtrades).

## What is it?

The **Logic BigTrades** indicator paints large institutional orders directly on your chart in real time. Each dot, line, or bubble represents an aggressive trade above a configurable size threshold — letting you see exactly when and where the big players are entering the market.

This is the closest you can get to watching the institutional order flow live, without needing access to the tape.

## Key features

- **Configurable size threshold** to filter out retail noise
- **Bid/Ask coloring** to instantly see whether the aggressor was a buyer or seller
- **Multiple visualization styles** (dots, lines, bubbles)
- **Audio alerts** when a trade above threshold hits your chart
- **Historical replay** for post-session review

## Who is it for?

Traders who base their decisions on aggressive order flow and want to react in real time when a large participant enters the market.
