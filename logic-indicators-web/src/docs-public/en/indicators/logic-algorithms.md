## What Problem Does It Solve?

Traditional Order Flow analysis places an overwhelming visual and cognitive demand on traders:

1. **Information Overload and Mental Fatigue:** A standard Footprint chart packs hundreds of figures per candle. Trying to identify 3 stacked imbalances, delta divergences, passive absorption walls, and wick exhaustion simultaneously leads to analysis paralysis and delayed entries.
2. **Lagging Signals from Conventional Indicators:** Traditional oscillators (RSI, MACD, Stochastic) rely on past price averages; they inevitably lag behind. By the time they signal a reversal, the institutional push has already played out.
3. **Inability to Spot Traps in Real Time:** Retail traders often chase breakout candles at highs because they appear strong, unaware that large institutional players passively absorbed every aggressive market buy at that exact tick, trapping breakout buyers.

It completely automates microstructural market reading. Its algorithmic engine scans every executed contract and plots clear, customizable visual cues for the **11 most profitable auction events in professional trading**: limit order absorptions, trapped buyers and sellers, unfinished auctions, and multi-level diagonal imbalances.
