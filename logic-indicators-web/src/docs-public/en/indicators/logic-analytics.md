# Logic Analytics

> **Public overview** — for the complete technical manual, please [sign in to your account](/login?next=/dashboard/docs/indicators/logic-analytics).

## What is it?

The **Logic Analytics** indicator lets you draw custom statistical boxes directly on your chart to analyze any specific area of price action — a swing, a session, a consolidation, or a single event. It instantly calculates Volume, Delta, High/Low, Range, and other key statistics for the area you selected.

It's the perfect post-session tool for understanding the auction process of a particular move without having to manually crunch the numbers.

## Key features

- **Drag-and-drop boxes** over any area of the chart
- **Real-time recalculation** as you resize the selection
- **Volume, Delta, Range, High/Low, and more** displayed in a clean stat panel
- **Bid/Ask split** inside the box for aggressive buyer vs. seller analysis
- **Multiple boxes** can coexist for side-by-side comparison

## Who is it for?

Traders who do post-session review and want to quickly extract statistics from any specific area of price action without exporting data to spreadsheets.
