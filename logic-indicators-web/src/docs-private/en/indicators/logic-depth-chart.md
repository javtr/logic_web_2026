# Logic Depth Chart

> If you haven't installed the Logic Indicators suite yet, please check the [Installation Guide](/docs/installation) first.

The **Logic Depth Chart** indicator is the Level 2 institutional liquidity map integrated directly into price action for NinjaTrader 8.

Take your Order Flow analysis to the next level by uncovering the intentions of major participants before price moves. Logic Depth Chart for NinjaTrader 8 tracks and renders historical passive liquidity directly behind and across every candle on your chart, paired with a visual live order book profile.

## Key Features

1. **Passive Liquidity Tracking:** Renders historical liquidity in real time directly over your chart.
2. **Live Order Book Profile:** Visualize institutional contracts placed, modified, or absorbed.
3. **Native Integration:** Engineered for zero latency in NinjaTrader 8.
